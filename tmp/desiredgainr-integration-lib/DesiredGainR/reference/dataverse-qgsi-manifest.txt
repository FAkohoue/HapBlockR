===================================

Pérez-Rodríguez P, Fernandes SB, Ortiz R, Crossa J (2026). Replication Data

Important script variants preserved in the comparison:

* the simulated QGSI script constructs BB = P^-1 G A G P^-1;
* the real-data QGSI script reads A as an already constructed quadratic
  coefficient matrix and sets BB = A;
* both evaluate the quadratic variance contribution as
  2 tr(BB P BB P), with the corresponding merit term 2 tr(A G A G).

The deposit omits several intermediate CSV/TXT matrices referenced by the
scripts. DesiredGainR therefore labels its current validation as an
implementation-level reproduction of the deposited arithmetic, not a complete
rerun of every published numeric result.
