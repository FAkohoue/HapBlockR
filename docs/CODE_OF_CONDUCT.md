# Code of conduct

HapBlockR is committed to a respectful, inclusive, and professionally
constructive community.

Participants must communicate with care, welcome relevant experience
from breeders, quantitative geneticists, data stewards, software
engineers, and students, and criticise ideas or evidence rather than
people. Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, deliberate disclosure
of private information, and sustained disruptive behaviour are
unacceptable.

Project maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and may
temporarily or permanently restrict participation when conduct threatens
the safety, dignity, or effective work of the community. Reports may be
sent privately to the package maintainer at `akohoue.f@gmail.com`.
Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical, with conflicts
of interest avoided.

This policy applies in project repositories, issue trackers, reviews,
community meetings, and other spaces where a person represents
HapBlockR.
